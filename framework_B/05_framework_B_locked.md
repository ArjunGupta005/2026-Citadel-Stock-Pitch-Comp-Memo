# Framework B — Citadel-criteria framework (LOCKED)

**Created:** 2026-10-01 (late evening ET), *after* Framework A results. **Framework A and every result produced under it (Rounds 1-3, the audit) are preserved unchanged** and remain on file. Framework B is a separate, dated rule set. It does not re-score or overwrite Framework A.
**Sources used exclusively:** (1) HFAC x Citadel "What Judges Look For in Your Memo" (Investment Thinking, Research, Financial Analysis, Risk & Balance, Catalysts; "greatest emphasis on a differentiated thesis backed by analysis"). (2) Submission requirements (2-page blinded memo incl. appendices; supporting model; one listed ticker; Long or Short; 3-12 months). (3) Citadel info-session Q&A guidance (transcript excerpts supplied by the team, 2026-10-01).
**Why a second framework:** Framework A's fixed 2:1 ratio gate is not part of Citadel's criteria. Framework B replaces the ratio gate with the disclosure-and-evidence requirements Citadel actually describes. Any pitch chosen under B will openly state its Framework A result.

## Gates (all required; any failure = KILL)

| # | Gate | Operational test | Citadel source |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Differentiated** | Name what consensus believes, with evidence (consensus numbers, sell-side framing, management guidance), and show the thesis differs on a specific, measurable item. "Well-researched but already in consensus" fails. | "greatest emphasis on a differentiated thesis"; Q: differentiated vs already reflected in consensus |
| B2 | **Why will the market come around** | A named mechanism plus a discrete, dated 3-12 month event. Map the event day: what is disclosed, what the market learns that it does not know today, and the expected reaction. | "why are people going to come around to your view"; "discrete events… map out what that day is going to look like" |
| B3 | **Quantified fundamental bridge** | Earnings-revision and valuation-regime theses are both allowed. **Every multiple change must be bridged to a quantified fundamental driver** (growth duration, margin, capital intensity, cash conversion, risk). A multiple change without a bridge fails. | "stocks are driven by numbers and narrative… what does that mean for numbers? what does that mean for multiple?" |
| B4 | **Thoughtful scenario distribution** | ≥3 economically distinct worlds (not ±1%). Each world specifies what the market thinks → KPIs → numbers → multiple → price, with explicit probabilities summing to 1 and a probability-weighted value. | Q&A on scenario analysis (auto-supplier example) |
| B5 | **Explicit maximum downside** | State "if we're wrong, how far can we be wrong": the adverse-world price and the KPI sensitivity (e.g. KPI X at value Y vs Z → price impact). | "truly map out if you're wrong, how far could you be wrong" |
| B6 | **Falsification threshold** | A specific KPI value, observable within the horizon, at which we would exit or flip. | "what would make you take the other side" |
| B7 | **Full opposing thesis** | Write the best opposite-side pitch: 3 reasons for each side, the opposing valuation and catalysts, and a judgement on which side explains the evidence better. Idiosyncratic, not generic macro. | PM practice: "three reasons long, three reasons short… debate both sides" |
| B8 | **Actionable and positive expectancy** | Probability-weighted return in the thesis direction > 0 after the B5 downside, and the position can be sized (stated max loss at a stop / falsification). There is **no fixed ratio hurdle**; the ratio is disclosed, not gated. | Risk/reward "incredibly important… overlooked" |
| B9 | **Data integrity** | Primary sources (10-Q/10-K/8-K/calls) for every critical datapoint; consensus labelled secondary with timestamp; fiscal period / GAAP / share-count alignment checked (lessons from the Framework A audit). | Research: "Variant views are backed by supporting research" |
| B10 | **Model aligned with thesis** | The KPI driving the thesis is the KPI driving the model; assumptions trace to sources. | Financial Analysis: "Model assumptions make sense and align with the thesis" |

## Process rules
- Investigate before choosing a direction. Direction is chosen only after the fundamental work (B1, B3) is complete.
- Write a pre-valuation card (evidence for/against, expected observations if true/false, KPI, falsification, catalyst, information revealed) and **freeze it (hash) before valuation**.
- Backtests may kill (robust contradiction) but cannot prove (small n is supporting only).
- Kill if the market already understands the variant (B1) or the stock is not priced on the metric in question.
