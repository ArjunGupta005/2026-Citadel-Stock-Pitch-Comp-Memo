## 1. What has management said?
- "Back to Starbucks" is working: four straight quarters of positive comps, led by transactions (+4.5% NA).
- FY27 will be "an important modernization year" with new inventory, staffing, scheduling and POS systems.
- North America unit growth will be "modest through fiscal 2027". There are more closures of underperforming stores: about 250, with $300m of charges, announced 9/24.
- Coffee cost pressure is easing and will be "largely immaterial" to y/y margin by Q4. The $2bn gross savings plan runs through FY28.
- On tariff refunds, the YTD COGS rate is "the better proxy".

## 2. What does guidance imply?
- FY26 EPS of $2.55-2.65 with Q1-Q3 at $1.91 (0.56 + 0.50 + 0.85) implies **Q4 of about $0.64-0.74** (midpoint $0.69, vs Street $0.71).
- FY26 margin above 11%.
- FY27 has no guidance yet; it is due at Q4 on about 10/29/26.

## 3. What is the Street expecting?
- Q4 EPS is $0.71 (+36% y/y).
- FY27 EPS is $3.12 (+20%), revised up from $3.01 90 days ago. FY27 revenue is $38.6bn (+1.5%; the China JV deconsolidation depresses reported revenue).

## 4. Where is the disagreement?
- **Margin normalization vs. +20% EPS.** FY27 consensus needs a further ~250bp+ of operating margin on roughly flat revenue. Q3's 14.4% included about 80-110bp of one-time refunds. FY27 also carries modernization costs and closures, and laps +8% comps in 2H.
- **The opposite side.** Coffee deflation, $2bn of savings, G&A below FY23 levels, and transaction-led comps with operating leverage.

## 5. Which KPI explains it?
**North America operating margin** (with NA comp as its main driver). 100bp of consolidated margin is about $0.26 EPS; 1pt of NA comp is about $0.05 EPS. The disagreement is mainly about margin, not comps.
