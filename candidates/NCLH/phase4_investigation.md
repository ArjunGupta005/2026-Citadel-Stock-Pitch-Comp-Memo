## 1. What has management said?
- The problems are "self-inflicted" and come from marketing and demand generation at the NCL brand: "vast majority of our problems... are self-inflicted" (Chidsey, Q2 call). Regent and Oceania are "running well".
- They are moving to **base-loading** revenue management: competitive prices set earlier in the curve. They have "taken pricing initiatives on select sailings in 2027". The benefit is greatest for late-2027 and 2028 sailings.
- In 2027, 1H yields will be negative and worst in Q1; 2H will improve; **2028 is "the first normalized year."**
- On costs, $225m of annualized savings have been identified over two quarters, but **"the vast majority... relate to capital expenditures"**. NCC ex-fuel growth has been ≤1% for three years running.
- Capex falls by about $1bn a year from 2028 (one ship a year in 2028-29). Capacity CAGR is 2.5% for 2026-29.
- Leverage will be >6x at year-end 2026. There are no significant maturities until 2030.
- On 9/30, they said Q3 will beat on revenue, reaffirmed FY26, and set **FY27 net interest at $860-880m**, up from ~$705m in FY26. They issued $950m of 8.75% 2031 notes to refinance 6.125% 2028 notes.

## 2. What does guidance imply?
- FY26: Q3 -8.9% plus Q4 about -6.5%, with a pre-announced Q3 beat (perhaps ~1pt, consistent with history).
- FY27 interest is up about $165m y/y. On ~462m shares and near-zero tax, that is **about -$0.36 of EPS, all else equal**.
- 1H27 negative yields and base-loading point to FY27 yield of roughly flat at the midpoint (illustrative path: Q1 about -4%, Q2 about -1%, Q3 +2%, Q4 +3%).
- D&A rises with two 2026 deliveries (Luna in March 2026) and two in 2027.

## 3. What is the Street expecting?
- FY27 EPS is $1.67, up about 9% from FY26's $1.54. It has been cut 18% in 90 days, and the range is wide ($1.22-1.95).
- FY27 revenue is $10.57bn (+5.3%).
- Q4-26 EPS is -$0.08.
- We could not confirm whether FY27 estimates include the 9/30 interest guide. Only one estimate moved down in the last 7 days.

## 4. Where is the disagreement?
- **The Street vs. guidance arithmetic.** Consensus of $1.67 combined with the new interest guide needs FY27 EBITDA of about $2.79bn (+12%), which requires roughly +2.5-3% yield. Management has guided 1H27 negative.
- **The market vs. the industry.** CCL said on 9/29 that "June was an inflection point", that July-August accelerated, and that 2027 is 50% booked at record price and occupancy. NCLH rose only +3% that day against CCL +13% and RCL +7.5%, and fell after its own Q3 beat. The market is pricing NCLH's problem as structural.
- **Caribbean supply.** CCL said Caribbean industry capacity is up about 37% over 3 years into 2027. NCLH's 2027 recovery leans on the Caribbean and Great Stirrup Cay.

## 5. Which KPI explains it?
**FY27 Net Yield (constant currency).** Each 1pt is about $0.16 EPS, about 10% of FY27 consensus. It is reported quarterly and guided each February (FY) and quarterly. Secondary: FY27 net interest (now guided) and occupancy (the base-loading trade-off).
