# 00 — Judging Framework (LOCKED)

**Locked:** 2026-10-01
**Purpose:** Grading rubric only. Do **not** use this to generate or screen ideas.
It is applied after a thesis exists, to grade it.

## Competition criteria

1. Differentiated thesis
2. Research supporting the variant view
3. Financial model aligned with thesis
4. Risk / opposite side considered
5. Detailed catalyst path
6. 3–12 month horizon

## Q&A guidance

7. Why now?
8. What is actually actionable?
9. What would make us take the other side?
10. What causes the market to come around to our view?
