## ADBE-1: Freemium funnel is masking paid-seat decay (KPI composition) — direction: SHORT
**Thesis:** The 36-37% y/y fall in net new ARR is presented as a deliberate freemium trade-off, but it reflects slower paid conversion. The first FY27 targets under a new CEO will set Total Adobe ARR growth well below FY26's 10.2%.

Evidence FOR:
- Net new ARR down 36-37% y/y in Q3-FY26 (analyst question, not disputed by management). *ADBE Q3-FY26 call, 2026-09-10*
- Management redirected "a portion of our traffic" to the freemium funnel and deferred pricing actions. *Same call*
- cRPO growth about 9% vs reported revenue +13%, so forward contracted revenue is growing slower than reported revenue. *Same call*
- The FY26 ending-ARR guide of +10.2% vs +11.2% exiting Q3 implies Q4 deceleration. *Release / call*
- New CEO from 12/1 with an interim CFO: first-year targets are typically set conservatively. (Behavioral prior; weak.)

Evidence AGAINST:
- AI-first ARR exceeds $650m, growing >150% y/y; creative freemium MAU >100m (+70%); total MAU >1bn (+20%). *Call*
- BP&C subscription revenue +16%; Q3 revenue and EPS beat; FY26 targets raised. *Release 2026-09-10*
- Q4 is seasonally the strongest net-new-ARR quarter (enterprise). *Call*
- The deferred pricing actions are a latent lever the new CEO could pull in FY27.

What would we expect to observe if thesis is true?
- FY27 ending-ARR growth target ≤8.0%; FY27 revenue growth target <8.5%; Q4-FY26 net new ARR down y/y again.

What would we expect if thesis is false?
- FY27 ARR target ≥9.5%; Q4 net new ARR flat or up y/y; announced price actions lifting ARR.

Primary KPI: FY27 Total Adobe ending-ARR growth target (company-defined).
Falsification threshold: target ≥9.5% falsifies; ≤8.0% confirms; 8.0-9.5% is inconclusive.
Catalyst: Q4-FY26 results + FY27 targets, est. 2026-12-09 (Yahoo calendar; secondary).
Expected information revealed at catalyst: first FY27 ARR, revenue and EPS targets under the new CEO; Q4 net new ARR.

## ADBE-2: Per-share compounding through buybacks is mispriced (capital allocation) — direction: LONG
**Thesis:** At about a 10%+ FCF yield, Adobe's buyback shrinks the share count about 6-7% a year. That alone delivers double-digit EPS growth even if revenue growth slows to about 7-8%. The market prices the stock as if EPS will stall.

Evidence FOR:
- Diluted shares fell from 424.1m to 395.5m y/y (−6.7%). *XBRL, Q3-FY26 10-Q*
- About 9.5m shares repurchased in Q3; $24.55bn left on the April-2026 authorization (~25% of the ~$96bn market cap). *Q3 call*
- Record Q3 operating cash flow of $2.52bn; FY26 non-GAAP operating margin ~45%. *Release*

Evidence AGAINST:
- A new CEO could redirect cash to M&A (e.g. the Topaz Labs deal; AI acquisitions), slowing buybacks.
- If revenue decelerates (ADBE-1), margin leverage may reverse; FY27 consensus already models +13% EPS. *Yahoo*
- Buyback-driven EPS growth is often not rewarded with a higher multiple when organic growth slows.

What would we expect to observe if thesis is true?
- FY27 non-GAAP EPS target ≥$27.4 (≥+12% vs FY26 ~$24.5) with diluted shares down ≥6% y/y in Q4-FY26 and Q1-FY27.

What would we expect if thesis is false?
- FY27 EPS target <$26.7 (<+9%) or a visible slowdown in repurchases (<6m shares per quarter).

Primary KPI: FY27 non-GAAP EPS target; diluted share count.
Falsification threshold: EPS target midpoint <$26.7 or Q4-FY26 repurchases <6m shares.
Catalyst: Q4-FY26 + FY27 targets, est. 2026-12-09.
Expected information revealed at catalyst: FY27 EPS and share-count assumption; capital-allocation stance of the new CEO.

## ABNB-1: GBV growth is shifting to low-take-rate sources (KPI composition) — direction: SHORT
**Thesis:** Hotels (growing 3x homes, listed on an "extremely favorable" take rate), new-business incentives (15% credits, price matching) and pass-through services mean revenue will grow slower than GBV in 2027. The implied take rate will decline once FX and RNPL timing stop flattering reported revenue.

Evidence FOR:
- Hotels grow about 3x faster than homes and are attracted by Airbnb's "extremely favorable take rate". *ABNB Q2-26 call (CEO)*
- FY26 take rate guided flat *despite* monetization initiatives, because of "higher customer incentives related to new businesses". *Q2 call (CFO)*
- Q2 revenue +17% reported vs +13% ex-FX; GBV +16% (+15% ex-FX). *Q2-26 shareholder letter*
- The single host-side service fee rolls out to all hosts by end-2026, positioned to help hosts "price more competitively". *Q2 call*

Evidence AGAINST:
- Management: absent incentives, the take rate would have been "slightly higher". *Q2 call*
- Hotels are still a single-digit % of nights. Services are partner-based with low incremental cost.
- Q2 take rate flat y/y (~13.2-13.3%). FY26 EBITDA margin raised to ≥35.5%.
- RNPL shifts unearned fees into Q3, so Q3 may *look* strong.

What would we expect to observe if thesis is true?
- Q3-26 implied take rate (revenue / GBV) below Q3-25. Q4 guide shows revenue growth below GBV growth. 2027 commentary (Feb) points to take-rate pressure.

What would we expect if thesis is false?
- Q3 take rate ≥ Q3-25 and Q4 revenue growth ≥ GBV growth.

Primary KPI: implied take rate (revenue / GBV), Q3-26 vs Q3-25.
Falsification threshold: Q3-26 take rate ≥ Q3-25 take rate.
Catalyst: Q3-26 results and Q4 outlook, 2026-11-05 (Yahoo calendar; secondary). Q4 + 2027 outlook, est. mid-Feb-27.
Expected information revealed at catalyst: Q3 GBV and revenue split; incentive cost; first 2027 color.

## SPOT-1: Gross-margin expansion is plateauing (margin structure) — direction: SHORT
**Thesis:** Price increases are increasingly shared with rights holders (marketplace programs, AI-feature revenue shares) and reinvested (~€200m opex). Gross margin stalls around 33%, below the trajectory consensus needs for +26% FY27 EPS.

Evidence FOR:
- Q3-26 gross-margin guide 32.9% vs Q2 33.4% (sequential decline). *6-K 2026-08-04, Outlook*
- Secondary report: revenue sharing on the AI cover-version feature offsets more of the pricing benefit than expected. *Press; unverified*
- €200m of incremental 2026 opex for AI and marketing. *Press summary of Q2 call*
- Ad-supported revenue +1% y/y in Q2 (weak high-margin mix lever). *6-K*

Evidence AGAINST:
- The Q3 guide of 32.9% is still +130bp y/y (Q3-25: 31.6%). *6-K*
- Premium ARPU +7% y/y; subscribers beat; LTM FCF €3.3bn. *6-K*
- Q2 GM was a record 33.4% and beat guidance by about 30bp. Spotify has beaten GM guidance recently.

What would we expect to observe if thesis is true?
- Q3 GM ≤33.0% and a Q4-26 GM guide ≤33.3%; FY27 commentary emphasises reinvestment.

What would we expect if thesis is false?
- Q3 GM ≥33.3% (a beat) and a Q4 guide ≥33.6%.

Primary KPI: gross margin (Q3 actual, Q4 guide).
Falsification threshold: Q4-26 GM guide ≥33.6%.
Catalyst: Q3-26 results + Q4 guide, 2026-10-22 (company-confirmed date).
Expected information revealed at catalyst: Q3 GM vs 32.9% guide; Q4 GM guide; reinvestment plans.

## GEV-1: Slot-reservation backlog extends earnings duration beyond 2028 (duration of growth) — direction: LONG
**Thesis:** Gas slot reservations (63 GW + 53 GW backlog, at least 125 GW by YE26) and capacity going to 30 GW by 2030 lock in pricing and volume past the 2028 targets. The December investor update will raise the 2028 framework or add 2030.

Evidence FOR:
- Q2-26 orders +88% organic; backlog $176bn; gas backlog + slots 100 → 116 GW in one quarter; target ≥125 GW at YE26. *8-K 2026-07-22*
- Electrification book-to-bill about 1.7; data-center orders >$5bn YTD. *Same*
- FY26 guide raised (revenue $45.5-46.5bn; FCF $11.5-12.5bn). *Same*
- Capacity plan 20 GW (Q3-26) → 24 GW (2028) → 30 GW (2030). *Same*

Evidence AGAINST:
- Q2 adjusted EBITDA margin 11.3% vs the 2028 target of 20%: the target already requires a steep ramp.
- Wind EBITDA losses are growing (offshore project costs).
- 2028 targets were already raised in Dec-2025 ($45bn → $52bn revenue, 14% → 20% margin), so the market may expect another raise.

What would we expect to observe if thesis is true?
- The December update raises 2028 revenue above $52bn and/or margin above 20%, or introduces 2030 targets above trend; Q3 orders stay above revenue.

What would we expect if thesis is false?
- 2028 targets merely reaffirmed; slot conversions slow; Q3 orders decline y/y.

Primary KPI: 2028 adjusted EBITDA (revenue × margin target).
Falsification threshold: 2028 targets unchanged or lowered at the December update.
Catalyst: Q3-26 results, 2026-10-28 (Yahoo; secondary); 2026 investor update, est. early-mid Dec (Dec-9 in 2025).
Expected information revealed at catalyst: new multi-year targets; pricing on new slots.

## GEV-2: FCF is customer financing, not earnings power (cash quality) — direction: SHORT
**Thesis:** FY26 FCF guidance ($11.5-12.5bn, about 2x adjusted EBITDA) is driven by slot-reservation deposits: contract liabilities went from $20.3bn to $40.0bn in nine months. As reservations convert and deliveries ramp, deposit inflows slow. The FY27 FCF guide falls below FY26, breaking the "FCF compounder" narrative.

Evidence FOR:
- Contract liabilities: $20.3bn (2025-09-30) → $25.95bn → $31.95bn → $40.0bn (2026-06-30). *10-Q / 10-K XBRL*
- Q2 FCF of $5.1bn was "primarily due to higher positive benefits from working capital". *8-K 2026-07-22*
- FY26 FCF guide raised by $5bn while the EBITDA margin guide was unchanged (12-14%). *Same*

Evidence AGAINST:
- Deposits keep rising as long as orders grow; the YE26 target of at least 125 GW implies continued inflows.
- Cumulative FCF and backlog guidance have been raised repeatedly; there is no sign yet of a deposit slowdown.
- Equity value depends on long-run earnings, not single-year FCF. The price effect of an FCF reset is a narrative / multiple effect (R8 risk).

What would we expect to observe if thesis is true?
- Q3-26 increase in contract liabilities <$4bn; FY27 FCF guide below the FY26 midpoint ($12bn).

What would we expect if thesis is false?
- Q3 increase in contract liabilities ≥$5bn; FY27 FCF guide ≥$12bn.

Primary KPI: quarterly change in contract liabilities; FY27 FCF guide.
Falsification threshold: FY27 FCF guide ≥$12bn, or Q3 increase in contract liabilities ≥$5bn.
Catalyst: Q3-26 10-Q (contract liabilities), around 2026-10-28; investor update / FY27 guide, est. Dec.
Expected information revealed at catalyst: the deposit run-rate and the first FY27 FCF guide.

## SBUX-1: Japan majority-stake sale (capital structure) — KILLED AT DEFINITION
**Thesis considered:** A sale of a majority of Starbucks Japan (~$3bn valuation reported 9/16) moves SBUX further toward an asset-light licensor. The narrative re-rates the stock, while the deal is EPS-dilutive.

Evidence FOR: press reports of a ~$3bn valuation and advisers solicited (2026-09-16); the China JV precedent (60% sold, royalties retained); proceeds earmarked for debt reduction.
Evidence AGAINST: no company confirmation; a formal process only "potentially" starts in Q4.

**Kill reason:** it cannot be translated into a model and stock-price implication.
1. Japan revenue and operating income are not separately disclosed (10-K reports the U.S., China and "other"), so EPS dilution cannot be estimated from primary data.
2. There is no dated catalyst: signing could fall outside the 12-month window.
3. The price implication would be a pure narrative or multiple effect (R8).

No second SBUX hypothesis was proposed: every other structural angle examined (comp composition, closures, margin flow-through) overlapped the Round-1 SBUX hypotheses, which are not to be reopened.
