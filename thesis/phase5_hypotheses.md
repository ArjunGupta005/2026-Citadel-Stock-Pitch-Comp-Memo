# Phase 5 — Hypotheses (pre-evidence, direction NOT decided)

Required form: *Consensus believes X. We believe Y because Z. If correct, KPI A moves to B, which changes earnings by C and the stock by D.*
Numbers are **preliminary** (Phase 4 arithmetic). Phase 6 tests them, Phase 7 replaces them with model outputs.
Consensus is yfinance as of 2026-10-01 unless noted. "Stock by D" holds today's forward P/E constant unless noted, so D is an EPS effect, not a re-rating.

Each hypothesis is listed with its opposite where one exists, so neither direction is presumed.

## NCLH ($14.64; FY27 cons. EPS $1.67)

**N-A: FY27 yield recovery is over-estimated (short-leaning)**
Consensus believes FY27 EPS of $1.67. With the 9/30 interest guide ($860-880m), that needs FY27 EBITDA of about $2.79bn and Net Yield of about +2.5%. We believe FY27 Net Yield is about 0% (±1pt) because management guided 1H27 negative, base-loading lowers 2027 prices, and Caribbean industry capacity is up about 37% over 3 years. If correct, the FY27 Net Yield guide (late Feb-27) is about 0% vs about +2.5% implied. EPS is about $1.30 (-22% vs Street) and the stock about $11.40 (-22%).
*Ready: yes (implied-yield back-solve to be confirmed in Phase 7).*

**N-B: The market prices a structural problem; it is cyclical and company-fixable (long-leaning)**
Consensus believes NCLH's demand problem is structural: it lagged CCL by 10pts on 9/29 and trades at 8.8x vs RCL 13.2x. We believe the industry tide (CCL: June inflection, July-August acceleration), NCLH's conservative guiding (beats next-Q yield guide 79% of the time) and easy comparisons (Q3-26 -8.9%, Q4 about -6.5%) produce FY27 yield of +2-3%. If correct, the FY27 guide is ≥+2%, EPS is $1.67-1.80 (0 to +8% vs Street), and the P/E re-rates toward 11x for a stock price of about $19 (+30%).
*Ready: yes. Note: most of D is multiple expansion, so R8 must be checked.*

**N-C: Estimates have not absorbed the 9/30 interest guide**
Consensus believes FY27 EPS of $1.67. We believe estimates that predate 9/30 use about $705-760m of interest against the new $860-880m. If correct, the stale estimates fall by $0.25-0.36 (-15-22%).
*Ready: numerically yes. **But this is an information-lag trade, not a variant view**: it closes as soon as analysts update. It is kept as context only (R10).*

**N-D: Deleveraging is later than consensus assumes**
Consensus believes leverage peaks above 6x in 2026 and falls from 2027. We believe net leverage stays ≥6x through 2027 because EBITDA is about flat while two 2027 deliveries keep capex high, and the 8.75% coupon shows the credit market agrees. If correct, YE27 net leverage is about 6.2x vs about 5.5x implied. EPS is about -$0.05 from higher interest; the equity effect is through the multiple.
*Ready: partially. The EPS effect is small, so the variant runs through the multiple (R8 risk).*

## CPRT ($27.29; Q1-FY27 cons. EPS $0.397; FY27 $1.75)

**C-A: The full run-rate of the Progressive loss is under-modeled (short-leaning)**
Consensus believes Q1-FY27 EPS of $0.397 (-3% y/y) and FY27 EPS of $1.75 (+11%). We believe Q1 EPS is about $0.34-0.35 (-15%) and FY27 about $1.45. The reasons: the Progressive loss (about 12% of US insurance units) runs at full rate Aug-26 to Apr-27, against a partial Q4; OpEx/car inflation (+12.7%) persists; and interest income falls about $75m a year after ACV. If correct, Q1 US insurance units come in around -12% to -15% (Q4: -7.5%), EPS is -12% vs Street for Q1 and -17% for FY27, and the stock is about $23 (-15%).
*Ready: yes. Unit-loss sizing to be validated in Phase 6.*

**C-B: The volume trough is cyclical and lapping is near (long-leaning)**
Consensus believes the unit decline is structural, which explains the de-rating to 15.6x from a historical 25-35x. We believe the underlying insurance cycle is turning (US insurance ex-customer +2.3% in Q4, record total-loss frequency, severity +8.8%) and that the Progressive loss laps in Q4-FY27. If correct, ex-customer US insurance units run +2-4% and FY28 EPS growth resumes (+10%+). The stock re-rates to about 22x FY28 for about $36 (+32%).
*Ready: partially. The catalyst (lapping) arrives at the 12-month edge (Sep-27 print). D is mostly multiple, so R8 risk.*

**C-C: ACV is GAAP-dilutive in FY27, not "breakeven" (short-leaning, additive to C-A)**
Consensus (most models exclude ACV until close) believes ACV is neutral. We believe ACV is GAAP-dilutive by about $0.06-0.10 in FY27. ACV runs about -$57m TTM operating income; purchase amortization is about $50-80m a year on a $1.9bn price; and about $75m of interest is lost. Copart reports GAAP EPS only. If correct, FY27 GAAP EPS is about $0.08 lower once ACV is consolidated (from Q2/Q3-FY27), and the stock about -5%.
*Ready: yes. Amortization is an assumption pending the purchase-price allocation.*

**C-D: Cost-per-car inflation is structural (short-leaning)**
Consensus believes OpEx/car normalizes, with Q1 EBITDA of about $490m (+4%). We believe facility cost/unit keeps growing ≥8% because the new services (long-haul, TitleExpress, wholesale facilities) are being scaled and "you can extrapolate". If correct, US gross margin stays below 44% (Q4: 43.4%), operating margin stays at about 32-33% vs 36-37% a year ago, and EPS is about -8%.
*Ready: yes. It overlaps C-A (the same quarter tests both).*

## SBUX ($94.88; FY27 cons. EPS $3.12)

**S-A: FY27 margin expansion is over-estimated because Q3 was flattered by refunds (short-leaning)**
Consensus believes FY27 EPS of $3.12 (+20%), which implies about 13.5% non-GAAP operating margin (FY26 about 11.7%). We believe about 12.7%. Q3's 14.4% included about 80-110bp of IEEPA refunds that "largely offset" three quarters of tariffs. FY27 is a "modernization year" (POS, inventory, labor systems). Store closures and 2H-FY27 comparisons against +8% comps limit leverage. If correct, the FY27 operating margin guide (around 10/29) is about 12.5-13% vs 13.5% implied, EPS is about $2.90 (-7%), and the stock about $88 (-7%).
*Ready: yes. Magnitude is near the R1 edge (5%).*

**S-B: Transaction-led comps are durable and operating leverage surprises (long-leaning)**
Consensus believes comps normalize to about 3-4% in FY27 (revenue +1.5% reported). We believe NA comps hold ≥5% as transactions (+4.5%) compound with uplifts and Green Apron Service. If correct, the NA comp is ≥5% in 1H-FY27 and EPS about $3.30 (+6%). The stock is about $100 (+5%) at a constant multiple.
*Ready: yes. Small magnitude.*

**S-C: The coffee-cost tailwind is under-appreciated (long-leaning)**
Consensus believes coffee is neutral in FY27. We believe falling arabica prices (to be checked) give 50-100bp of COGS tailwind in FY27. If correct, the FY27 margin guide is ≥14% and EPS about $3.30 (+6%).
*Ready: **no**. Coffee price data and the hedge lag are not yet sourced.*
